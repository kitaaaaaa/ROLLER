# Arena mode

ROLLER-X 0.2.0 adds an arcade mecha arena fighter to the racing game. It runs on
ROLLER's own software rasteriser and its 14-bit angle math, reached with
`--arena` or from the main menu, and it needs no assets of its own: the
machines, the arenas, the projectiles and the HUD font are all generated at
runtime, and every texture comes out of Whiplash's FATDATA as the mode loads.

## The fight

Sixty ticks a second, independent of the race game's own loop. Boost pays for
dashing, hovering and the air dash, and a machine with an empty gauge can still
jump and jump-cancel — it just has no thrust to spend. Locking on turns the body
without turning the travel, so a machine dashing across an opponent keeps its
line while it shoots. Launch off a cambered surface and the machine rolls in the
air the way Whiplash's cars do; land on your roof and you are down.

Six arenas, a roster of machines built from the retail car plans, and computer
pilots that press forward and take the high ground rather than circling cover.

## Modes

- **Duel** — one on one, best of however many rounds you set.
- **Survival** — sixteen machines, every one on its own team, last one standing.
- **Spectator** — no machine of your own, flying ROLLER's free camera around the
  fight.

The briefing screen sets all of it up: machine, paint scheme, opponent, arena,
skill, round time, and the way back out to the race game. Paint schemes are
named for the makes that built Whiplash's cars.

## Notes

The arena reads textures from the retail Whiplash data; without it the mode
still runs, drawing its own flat colours and its own font.
